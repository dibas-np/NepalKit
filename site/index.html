NepalKit

Today's Bikram Sambat date in your Mac's menu bar. Click it to see the month, with festivals and public holidays marked.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 26.6 or later

brew install --cask dibas-np/tap/nepalkit

Click the date to see the month

Click the menu-bar date for today's Bikram Sambat date above a month grid, with each day's Gregorian date beneath it.

Festivals and national days

A dot marks each festival and national day of 2083, taken from Nepal's national Panchanga. The names of today's festivals appear under the date.

Public holidays in red

Nationwide public holidays from the Nepal Gazette are red, like Saturdays. A day's details also name holidays for a community, region, or group.

Details for any day

Click a day to see it in both calendars and how far it is from today. Copy it, or add it to your calendar as an all-day event.

Change months

Click the arrows, press the left and right arrow keys, or scroll over the grid. The title also shows which Gregorian months the Bikram Sambat month overlaps.

More in the Mac app

NepalKit runs in the menu bar and has no Dock icon.

Choose the menu-bar format

Add the weekday, the year, and the time, in 12- or 24-hour form, with or without seconds. Settings shows a live preview.

Nepal Time

A live UTC+5:45 clock, with your Mac's time beside it when the two differ. Today follows Nepal Time by default. If you choose your Mac's time zone instead, the two clocks swap places.

Date conversion

Convert between Bikram Sambat and Gregorian dates. The day picker offers only the days the chosen month has.

Digits and names

Latin or Devanagari digits, with Nepali or transliterated month and weekday names.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask Siri for today's, tomorrow's, or yesterday's date, or convert dates in either direction from Shortcuts.

Works offline

NepalKit needs no account and collects no analytics or telemetry. It uses the network only to check for updates.

Apple Watch app

A standalone Apple Watch app shows today's Bikram Sambat date, its weekday, and the Gregorian date. It works offline, without your Mac or iPhone.

  • Rectangular, inline, circular, and corner complications.
  • Follows Nepal Time in any time zone.
  • Refreshes at Nepal midnight.

The Watch app is a developer preview for watchOS 26 or later. It isn't on the App Store or in the Mac download. To install it, build it from source with Xcode.

Ask Siri

NepalKit answers with the Bikram Sambat date and weekday.

  • “What is today's Nepali date with NepalKit”
  • “What is tomorrow's Nepali date with NepalKit”
  • “What was yesterday's Nepali date with NepalKit”

NepalKit adds four actions to Shortcuts: today's date, tomorrow's or yesterday's date, Gregorian to Bikram Sambat, and Bikram Sambat to Gregorian.

Calendar data you can check

NepalKit covers 1975 through 2084 BS (13 April 1918 through 12 April 2028). Month lengths come from a bundled table, and the 2083 lengths match the government's national Panchanga. Festivals come from that Panchanga. Public holidays come from the Home Ministry's notice in the Nepal Gazette. SOURCES.md lists every source, correction, and limit. 2084 BS is a provisional projection. For official use, check the published Nepali Patro.
