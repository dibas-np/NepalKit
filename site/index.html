NepalKit

Today's Bikram Sambat date in your Mac's menu bar, with the whole month, its festivals and holidays a click away.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 26.6 or later

brew install --cask dibas-np/tap/nepalkit

The whole month, one click away

Click the menu-bar date for today's Bikram Sambat date above the month as a grid, with each day's Gregorian date beneath it.

Festivals and national days

2083's festivals and national days, from Nepal's national Panchanga, are marked with a dot and named under today's date.

Public holidays in red

Nationwide public holidays from the Nepal Gazette show in red like Saturdays. Holidays limited to a community, region or group are named too.

Any day, in detail

Click a day to see it in both calendars and how far it is from today. Copy it, or add it to your calendar as an all-day event.

Flip through months

Scroll over the grid or press the left and right arrow keys. The Gregorian months it spans sit beside the title.

Everything else you'd expect

A native menu-bar app with no Dock icon, built for anyone who keeps time by the Nepali calendar.

Your menu bar, your way

Add the weekday, the year, and the clock (12- or 24-hour, with or without seconds), with a live preview in Settings.

Nepal Time

A live UTC+5:45 clock beside your local time. Today follows Nepal Time by default, or your Mac's own time zone if you choose.

Date conversion

Convert between Bikram Sambat and Gregorian, with pickers that follow each month's actual length.

Your script, your names

Latin or Devanagari digits, with Nepali or transliterated month and weekday names.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask Siri for today's, tomorrow's or yesterday's date, or convert dates in either direction from Shortcuts.

Private by design

Works offline. No account, no analytics, no telemetry. The network is used only to check for updates.

On your wrist, too

A standalone Apple Watch app shows today's Bikram Sambat date with its weekday and the Gregorian date, offline and independent of your Mac and iPhone.

  • Rectangular, inline, circular, and corner complications.
  • Anchored to Nepal Time in any time zone.
  • Refreshes at Nepal midnight.

The Watch app is a developer preview for watchOS 26 or later. It isn't on the App Store or in the Mac download; you build it from source with Xcode.

Ask Siri

NepalKit answers with the Bikram Sambat date and weekday.

  • “What is today's Nepali date with NepalKit”
  • “What is tomorrow's Nepali date with NepalKit”
  • “What was yesterday's Nepali date with NepalKit”

Shortcuts adds five actions: today's, tomorrow's and yesterday's Bikram Sambat date, and conversion in both directions.

Calendar data you can check

NepalKit supports 1975 through 2084 BS (April 1918 through April 2028). Month lengths come from a bundled, sourced table, and 2083's are checked against the government's national Panchanga. Festivals come from that Panchanga, and public holidays from the Home Ministry's Nepal Gazette notice. Every source, correction and limit is documented in SOURCES.md. 2084 BS is a provisional projection. For official use, consult the published Nepali Patro.
