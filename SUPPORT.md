# Support

## Where to ask

**Issues** are the right place for bugs and feature requests:

- <https://github.com/dibas-np/NepalKit/issues>

Search first. A wrong date or a converter that will not convert is often already
reported.

**Do not use issues for security problems** — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting path.

## Before you file a bug

Most reports of "the date is wrong" are answered by one question: **what does
the official Nepali calendar say for that date?** The shipped table covers
1975–2084 BS and its provenance is documented in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md), which
also lists the months where the community sources disagree and which side was
taken.

Including what the official Patro says, alongside what the app shows, turns a
back-and-forth into a one-message resolution.

## What makes a good report

- NepalKit's version — `About NepalKit` shows it, with the build number in
  parentheses
- Your macOS version
- What you expected, and what happened instead
- Steps to reproduce, or the exact date you typed and what came back

## Using the app

- [README.md](README.md) covers installation, the menu-bar item, Settings, and
  the converter
- [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) explains the calendar data, its supported range, and
  why the range can narrow as well as extend

## There is no support chat

There is no Discord, no forum, and no mailing list. Issues and the repository
discussion are the whole of it. That is a deliberate trade for a project this
size, not an oversight.
